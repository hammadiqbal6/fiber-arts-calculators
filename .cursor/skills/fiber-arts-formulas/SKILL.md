description: Niche skill for yarn, gauge, quilt HST, binding, and Aida fabric math used in StitchCalc.
---
Prefer CYC standards. HST: finished+⅞" or 2×finished+1.75" for eight-at-a-time. Binding: perimeter+12". Aida: stitches/count. Always add unit tests beside formula changes.
